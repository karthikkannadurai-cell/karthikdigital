FAQ

## Common questions

Do you guarantee sales or leads?

No. Nobody honest can. We improve your chances with good targeting, creatives and fast follow-up, and we report real numbers.

Is the website assistant ChatGPT-style AI?

No. It is a rule-based assistant that asks set questions and prepares a WhatsApp enquiry. It does not use an LLM.

Who pays for the ads?

You pay Meta directly from your own ad account. Our fee is separate.

Do you work outside Cuddalore?

Yes, we work with businesses across Tamil Nadu online.

How soon can we start?

After a short discovery chat, setup usually begins within a few days.
